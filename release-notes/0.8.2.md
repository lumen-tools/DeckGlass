### Changed
- **Retro_Crisis and Custom_Shaders:** the libraries in the browser are now called **RetroArch**, **Retro_Crisis** and **Custom_Shaders**.
- **Custom_Shaders:** the custom library and its folder are now called `Custom_Shaders` (`/home/deck/DeckGlass/Custom_Shaders`) – no space in the path, so it is easier to use in a terminal or script.
- Presets in `~/DeckGlass/Custom Shaders` or the older `~/deckglass-shaders` are moved there automatically. Selected presets, per-game settings and save states keep working – also those pointing to Retro Crisis.
