### Changed
- **Source resolution presets are now based on the CRT raster: 240p and 480i/p.** TV consoles drew a 240-line (240p) or 480-line (480i/p) picture, and that is what CRT shaders look best with. The list is now:
  - **240p** – NES, SNES, Mega Drive, PS1, N64, Saturn, Arcade
  - **480i/p** – PS2, GameCube, Wii, Dreamcast
  - Handhelds with their real screens: Game Boy / Color, Game Boy Advance, Nintendo DS, PSP, Game Gear, Neo Geo Pocket, WonderSwan
- **The pixel size follows from the real height of the game picture.** DeckGlass sees the picture in the resolution the emulator renders, before gamescope scales it to the screen. Earlier versions assumed 800 lines, so a game rendered in 720p got the wrong factor. Now 240p always gets exactly 240 lines and 480i/p exactly 480: 720 lines → 3× / 1.5×, 800 lines → 3.33× / 1.67×.
- **Black bars top & bottom** is only needed when the emulator draws its own bars into an 800-line picture.
- Saved settings and per-game profiles are carried over (e.g. "SNES" becomes 240p, "PlayStation 2" becomes 480i/p).
