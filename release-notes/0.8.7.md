### Fixed
- **Black screen with crt-geom-deluxe and crt-geom-deluxe-ntsc-adaptive** at small pixel sizes (below about 2× or 2.8×). When an intermediate image gets larger than 262,144 pixels, its contents from the previous frame can be lost on the Deck, and the phosphor (afterglow) formula then computed "0 to a negative power", which turned the whole picture black. Powers are now computed safely; the picture is otherwise unchanged.

### Install
Download `DeckGlass-0.8.7.zip` below → Decky → Developer → **Install Plugin from ZIP**.
