Performance fix.

### Fixed
- **crt-geom-deluxe and similar presets were much slower since 0.2.2.** The emulated mipmaps made every screen pixel run a lookup through the whole mip chain, and the chain was built with 11 extra passes. Shaders that read a fixed mip level (crt-geom-deluxe reads the average brightness at level 9) now get it with a single texture fetch, and only the levels they need are built – up to three levels per pass. crt-geom-deluxe goes from 18 to 10 passes; the picture is the same.
- Shaders that compute their mip level while rendering (e.g. Retro Crisis' average luminance) keep the full chain, which they need.
